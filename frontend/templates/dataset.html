Research Overview

IEEE-oriented framing for the BE major project (linked with this dataset).

Research Gap

Prior EV forecasting rarely unifies temporal deep learning, explainability, and multi-state time-series analysis in one deployable system. This work addresses that with hybrid CNN-LSTM forecasting plus SHAP-based explanation on real registration / transaction / revenue data.

Literature Themes

  • Hybrid CNN-LSTM for temporal market signals
  • ARIMA / SARIMA classical time-series baselines
  • Random Forest / XGBoost tabular forecasting
  • SHAP / LIME for trustworthy AI
  • State-level spatial analysis of EV adoption
