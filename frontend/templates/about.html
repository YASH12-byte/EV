About the Project

Predictive Analytics for Electric Vehicle Market Growth using Hybrid Machine Learning and Time-Series.

Problem Statement

Existing EV market forecasting systems mainly rely on traditional statistical models or standalone ML algorithms that fail to capture complex spatial-temporal relationships, nonlinear market behavior, and dynamic policy impacts. This project develops a hybrid forecasting framework that is accurate, interpretable, scalable, and privacy-preserving.

Objectives

  1. Hybrid CNN-LSTM-Attention deep forecasting
  2. Robust preprocessing, denoising, and feature engineering
  3. Explainable AI with SHAP / LIME / attention maps
  4. Federated learning for cross-city privacy
  5. Physics-informed battery and grid constraints
  6. Rigorous comparison vs classical and ML baselines

Stakeholders

Governments, OEMs, investors, charging providers, and utilities can use the platform for capacity planning, policy evaluation, and investment decisions.
