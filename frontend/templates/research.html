Research Overview

IEEE-oriented framing for the BE major project.

Research Gap

Prior EV forecasting rarely unifies spatial-temporal deep learning, explainability, federated privacy, and physics-informed grid/battery constraints in one deployable system. This work closes that gap with a hybrid CNN-LSTM-Attention pipeline plus XAI and FedAvg cross-city training.

Literature Themes

  • Hybrid CNN-LSTM / CNN-GRU for temporal market signals
  • Transformers and TCN for long-range dependencies
  • STGCN / GNN for spatial charging networks
  • XGBoost / LightGBM / CatBoost tabular baselines
  • SHAP / LIME for trustworthy AI
  • Federated learning for multi-city collaboration
  • Physics-informed neural networks for constrained systems
